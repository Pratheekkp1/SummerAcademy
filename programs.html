Detailed Academic Programs Overview

Our academic tracks in Math, English, and Science are designed to inspire, challenge, and build confidence in every student. Each subject is organized by week, offering a clear and structured path for growth.

Science Program

The science curriculum is hands-on and inquiry-based, focusing on building a deep understanding of the natural world. Students explore life, earth, and physical sciences, with topics rotating each week for comprehensive exposure.

Grade Weekly Topics Overview
1 Living Things & Environments, Human Body, Matter, Measurement, Intro to Electricity, Astronomy, Earth, Scientists
2 Cycles in Nature, Insects, Human Body (Digestive & Excretory), Magnetism, Simple Machines, Science Biographies
3 Classification of Animals, Human Body (Muscular/Skeletal/Nervous), Vision & Hearing, Light & Optics, Sound, Ecology, Astronomy, Scientists
4 Human Body (Circulatory & Respiratory), Chemistry, Electricity, Geology, Meteorology, Science Biographies
5 Classifying Living Things, Cells, Plant Structures, Life Cycles, Reproduction, Human Body (Endocrine & Reproductive), Chemistry
6 Plate Tectonics, Oceans, Astronomy: Gravity/Stars/Galaxies, Energy/Heat/Transfer, Human Body: Lymphatic & Immune
7 Atomic Structure, Chemical Bonds & Reactions, Cell Division & Genetics, History of the Earth & Life, Evolution
8 Physics II, Electricity & Magnetism, Electromagnetic Radiation & Light, Sound Waves, Chemistry of Food & Respiration
Program Highlights:
  • Hands-on experiments and inquiry-based learning
  • Weekly focus on different branches of science
  • Biographies of scientists to inspire curiosity
